Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the link on your phone and add it to your home screen. It works like an app.

Where do the prices come from?

Store prices where stores share them, government averages for your region, and receipts you add. Each price shows its source and date.

I live outside the US. Can I use it?

Yes. Planning works everywhere, in your currency. Prices come from the receipts and prices you add.

Is it medical or nutrition advice?

No. Always read labels for allergies, and ask your doctor about special diets.
