# SamePlate (beta)

Family meal planning on a budget: a week of dinners with a kid-friendly plate for picky eaters, an aisle-sorted grocery list, and step-by-step recipes. English & Español.

Open the app: https://soberenough.github.io/sameplate/

Beta notes: prices are a snapshot from one Harris Teeter (Norfolk, VA). Everything you enter stays on your phone. Recipe photos are AI-generated illustrations.
